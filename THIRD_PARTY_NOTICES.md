# Third-Party Notices

## VCSL sample pack

The music workshop's bundled `assets/vcsl` sample pack is derived from the Versilian
Community Sample Library (VCSL), tag `v1.2.2-RC`, commit
`b6e6ac82d22248edee98a0bde185eb9ef6d439ad`.

VCSL is released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal. The full legal text is shipped
as `assets/vcsl/LICENSE.txt`; the source list and the exact instruments used are recorded
in `assets/vcsl/sources.json`.

The pack is a modified subset: samples were converted to mono 44.1 kHz OGG Vorbis, trimmed,
faded, and mapped to the workshop's instrument ids. VCSL by Versilian Studios is credited
as the sample source; no VCSL logo or other trademark asset is included, and this project
does not claim endorsement by Versilian Studios.

Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

The prebuilt browser bundle includes CodeMirror 6, Lezer, and their browser support libraries, including the declared `@codemirror/*` and `@lezer/*` dependencies plus `crelt`, `style-mod`, `w3c-keyname`, and `@marijn/find-cluster-break`.
